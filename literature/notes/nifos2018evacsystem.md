# nifos2018evacsystem

## Citation
윤호중 (Yoon, Ho-joong) [PI], 국립산림과학원 (National Institute of Forest Science).
「산림재해 피해저감을 위한 대피 및 경계피난시스템 개발」 / *Development of an Evacuation
System for Reducing Damages by Forest Disaster*. Research report to 산림청 (Korea Forest
Service), 2018. NTIS/ScienceON report number TRKO201800042755.

## Publication status
**GOVERNMENT_REPORT — NOT peer-reviewed.** Language: Korean. A commissioned agency research
report. Evidence level **E2** — the record abstract was read, the full text was not.

## Problem
Korean forest disasters (wildfire and debris flow) need a system that warns residents and
moves them, including residents who are hard to reach.

## Method
Per the record: spatial-information and meteorological modelling of smoke dispersion;
integrated hazard rating for debris flow used to rank warning-evacuation priority;
integration of unmanned remote monitoring, SNS-based real-time disaster monitoring, sirens
and cell broadcast; and disaster-type/region-specific standard operating procedures.

## Outputs
- 「산불연무 확산에 의한 대피범위 산정모델」 — an **evacuation-range estimation model**
  driven by wildfire smoke dispersion.
- 「토석류 통합위험도 판정기술을 이용한 경계피난 우선순위 선정」 — warning-evacuation
  **priority ranking**.
- 「산림재해 임의 취약자를 위한 위험정보 알림서비스」 — hazard-information alert service
  for vulnerable residents.
- 「사회적 취약계층을 위한 대피지원방안」 — evacuation **support measures** for socially
  vulnerable groups.

## Limitations
- Range, not time. The stated output is which area must clear, not when.
- "Support measures" for vulnerable groups is a policy category, not a modelled logistics
  quantity; nothing in the record indicates a vehicle, a trip, or a schedule.
- 2018, so it predates both the 2022 Uljin and 2025 Uiseong events and the 2025 5 h / 8 h
  rule.

## WildfireGuardian overlap
This is the earliest Korean government work found that couples a modelled hazard field to
an evacuation extent AND names socially vulnerable residents as a distinct planning object.
It occupies the "Korean agency thinks about vulnerable-resident wildfire evacuation" space.

## WildfireGuardian difference
NIFoS computes an evacuation **range** from modelled smoke; WildfireGuardian computes an
evacuation **deadline** from modelled fire-front arrival, on a round trip, for a named
responder. Different hazard variable, different output unit, different decision subject.

## Novelty threat
**MODERATE for WG-C-007 and WG-C-005.** It forbids "Korea has not considered vulnerable
residents in wildfire evacuation." It does not touch WG-C-003.
