# EXCLUSION_CRITERIA.md

Grounds for excluding a work. Every exclusion is recorded with its reason —
silent exclusion is how a literature review becomes an advertisement.

## X1 — Unverifiable
Cannot confirm the work exists through an independent source. **This is the
most important exclusion.** A remembered-but-unretrievable paper is a search
lead (`RECALL_UNVERIFIED`), never a citation.

## X2 — No decision or method content relevant to our claims
E.g. pure fire ecology, post-fire vegetation recovery, smoke-health
epidemiology, fire insurance pricing — unless it supplies I4 empirical
grounding.

## X3 — Predatory or non-reviewed venue masquerading as peer-reviewed
Excluded from the *evidence* base but, if it genuinely occupies a claim, still
recorded in `novelty/NOVELTY_THREATS.md` with its status disclosed. Venue
quality affects how much we trust its *results*; it does not affect whether the
idea is publicly prior.

## X4 — Duplicate
Preprint plus published version of the same work: keep one record, note both
identifiers, cite the published version, and record the preprint date as the
**priority date**.

## X5 — Generic ML/optimization with no hazard-decision content
A generic routing or forecasting paper with no connection to protective-action
decisions. **Careful:** this exclusion is easy to over-apply. A generic PDPTW
paper is excluded as *evidence* but is highly relevant as I3 occupancy if we
would otherwise claim the formulation. When in doubt, include.

## X6 — Superseded by the same authors
Where a later paper by the same group strictly supersedes an earlier one, keep
the later as primary; retain the earlier only for priority dating.

---

## Not grounds for exclusion

Stated explicitly because these are the temptations:

- **It threatens our claim.** Never. That is the reason to include it.
- **It is not in English.** Never.
- **It is only a preprint.** Never — preprints establish priority.
- **It is a low-prestige venue.** Never — see `NOVELTY_STANDARD.md` §3.3.
- **It is old.** Never.
- **We cannot get the full text.** Mark `NEEDS_FULL_TEXT`, do not exclude.
