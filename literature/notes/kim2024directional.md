## Problem
(Inferred from title only.) A community's ability to evacuate is not a single number: it
depends on which direction the fire comes from, because that determines which exits remain
usable.

## WildfireGuardian overlap
Sits directly on **WG-C-009 (robust protectability)**: "which approach directions can this
community survive?" is the same question as "which locations remain protectable under
model error," restated geometrically. Also adjacent to Kalogeropoulos's uncertainty
rosettes, which are likewise directional.

## WildfireGuardian difference
Provisional: Kim et al. appear to characterise a *community's* directional egress
capability, whereas WG-C-009 concerns *per-location protectability under fire-model
error* — uncertainty over the model, not over the approach azimuth. This distinction is a
hypothesis until the full text is read and must not be asserted in a defence.
